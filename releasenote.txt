🚀 Release Notes: Laravel Feature Kit v3.0.0
We are thrilled to announce the release of Laravel Feature Kit v3.0.0. This version represents a significant milestone in our journey toward providing the most professional and scalable starter kit for Laravel developers.

In this update, we’ve moved away from our custom "Feature-Driven" experimental structure to a Standardized, Professional MVC Architecture, while keeping all the powerful features you love.

🏛️ v3.0.0: The MVC Evolution
🏗️ Architectural Shift
We have completely refactored the backend to follow standard Laravel conventions. This ensures maximum compatibility with the Laravel ecosystem, superior IDE support, and a more intuitive experience for teams.

Unified Models: Models are now consolidated in app/Models/ as the single source of truth.
Role-Based Layering: Controllers, Services, and FormRequests are now organized by role (Admin/, User/, Auth/) within their respective standard Laravel directories.
Explicit Routing: We’ve retired the complex auto-discovery logic in favor of centralized, performant routing in routes/web.php and routes/api.php.
⚡ Performance & Lean Core
Faster Boot Times: Removing the recursive filesystem scan for routes significantly improves application performance.
Leaner Command Set: We have removed the 7 custom Artisan scaffolding commands (make:feature, etc.). This refocuses the kit as a high-quality Starter Kit rather than a scaffolding engine, reducing maintenance overhead and technical debt.
📊 Recap: What's in v2.2.0?
If you missed our previous update, v2.2.0 introduced the Advanced Traffic Analytics Console — a professional, self-hosted alternative to Google Analytics.

✨ Highlights
3-Tab Dashboard: Overview, Real-Time, and Behavior analytics.
Real-Time Monitoring: Live visitor stats polled every 15 seconds (no WebSockets required).
Traffic Heatmap: 7×24 hour intensity grid to identify peak traffic times.
Privacy First: Session tracking via privacy-safe hashes (GDPR-friendly, no cookies).
Geo-Tracking: Country detection with flag emojis via ip-api.com.
Performance Audit: Tracks per-request response time and HTTP status codes.
🛠️ How to Upgrade
As this is a major architectural overhaul, we recommend starting a fresh project and migrating your business logic into the new role-based folders.

Create a new project: composer create-project rifatxtra/laravel-feature-kit
Move your services to app/Services/{Role}/
Move your logic from feature controllers to app/Http/Controllers/{Role}/
Register your routes in routes/web.php
